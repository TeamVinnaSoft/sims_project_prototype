♙ Your Account

What is an account?

An account gives an authorized user access to the SIMS dashboard and the school management features assigned to them.

How do I secure my account?

Use a strong, unique password, keep your login details private, and log out when using a shared computer. Never share your password with others.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Contact your school's authorized administrator to request assistance with resetting your password.

▣ Features related

How can I activate some features?

Contact your school administrator to check whether the feature is available and whether your account has permission to use it.
