SIMS · Privacy and data protection

Your privacy matters to us

This page explains the general principles for handling information in the School Information Management System (SIMS). It describes how information may be collected, used, protected and managed when authorised users access the system.

Prototype policy page — please have your organisation review this content before publishing it as an official policy.

01 Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled by the school, SIMS may contain student and staff names, contact details, class information, subject details, attendance records, fee records and account information.

Information should be collected only when it is needed for an identified school or administrative purpose.

02 How We Use Information

Information may be used to support school administration and to:

  • Maintain student and staff records.
  • Manage attendance, classes and subjects.
  • Track fee-related information and prepare reports.
  • Provide authorised users with relevant dashboard features.

03 Data Security

Access to school information should be limited to authorised users. Appropriate safeguards may include secure passwords, role-based permissions, protected backups and removing access when a user no longer needs it.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their login details private and report suspected unauthorised access to the school administrator.

04 Sharing of Information

Personal information should be accessed or shared only by authorised people for legitimate school purposes, or when required by applicable law or an approved school process.

05 Data Retention and Accuracy

School records should be retained according to the school's approved procedures and applicable recordkeeping requirements. If a record is incorrect, contact an authorised administrator to request a correction.

06 Questions and Requests

For questions about information stored in SIMS, or to request access to or correction of a record, contact your school administrator or the person responsible for data protection.

Requests should be handled according to the school's procedures and applicable laws.
